Delete your Footies data

You can delete your online data from inside the app:

  1. Open Footies.
  2. Go to Privacy & support.
  3. Tap Delete my Footies data and confirm.

This deletes the anonymous online account, its League memberships and results, and the push-notification token, and clears local app data.

Delete in the app before deleting the app. League accounts in version 1.0 are anonymous — they are not tied to an Apple ID or an email address. Removing Footies from your iPhone clears local data but leaves online League data in place, and the anonymous identity cannot be recovered afterwards. If you need data deleted after removing the app, email the address above and describe your League name and display name so the records can be found.

Common questions

My daily progress disappeared

Progress is stored on the device. It does not transfer between iPhones, and it is removed if the app is deleted. Reinstalling does not restore previous streaks.

I'm not receiving notifications

Check that notifications are allowed for Footies in iOS Settings → Notifications → Footies, and that the topics you want are switched on inside the app. Alerts are opt-in for each topic separately.

A League code isn't working

League codes are three letters and are case-insensitive. Make sure every member is on the same app version and has a working internet connection when joining.

Why are clubs shown as shirts rather than badges?

Every club in Footies is drawn as its kit — the app draws the shirt itself from the club's colours. No club badge artwork is used anywhere in the app. This keeps Footies clear of the licensing that club crests carry, and means every club is presented the same way rather than some having a logo and others a placeholder.

Some games say "coming soon"

Who Are Ya?, Football Bingo, Fan Favourites, Guess the Club and Perfect Player are designed but not playable in this version. They are shown so the roster is honest about what is planned.

Is Footies available on iPad or Android?

Version 1.0 is iPhone only. The interface is a fixed phone-width layout, and iPad support will come only after a real tablet layout is built and tested.

Reporting a wrong answer

Footies builds puzzles from a public football dataset, and some records contain errors — a missing transfer, a wrong nationality, an outdated squad. If a puzzle looks wrong, email the game name, the date, and what you expected. These reports are the main way the data gets corrected.
