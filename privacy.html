Footies

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 August 2026

Footies is a football trivia and puzzle game. This policy explains the limited information used to operate the app.

Information stored on the device

Footies stores theme and favourite-club choices, notification preferences, game progress, hints, streaks, completed games and unfinished-game state on your device. This makes autosave, resume and personalisation work.

Information used by online features

Online League creates a random anonymous account identifier. Footies stores that identifier with the display name you choose, League membership, puzzle results, selected difficulty and completion time. This is used only to calculate and display League standings.

When you opt into notifications, Footies stores the device push token, selected notification topics, timezone, device language and, for favourite-club alerts, the selected club and fixture-feed team identifier. This is used only to send the requested Game of the Day, League and club-fixture alerts.

Our hosting and data providers may process network information such as IP address and request logs as needed to deliver and secure these services. Football fixture queries are made by the Footies server; the paid provider key is never included in the app.

Tracking, advertising and sale

Footies does not use advertising SDKs, sell personal information, or track users across apps or websites owned by other companies. Footies does not request contacts, precise location, photos, microphone or camera access.

Service providers

Footies uses Supabase for authentication, database, real-time League updates and server functions; Apple Push Notification service to deliver optional iPhone alerts; and a licensed football-fixture provider for selected club match times. These providers process information only as needed to provide their service, subject to their own terms and privacy commitments.

Retention and deletion

Online League information is retained while the anonymous Footies account exists. Push subscriptions are deleted when every notification topic is switched off.

You can open Privacy & support → Delete my Footies data inside the app to delete the online anonymous account, League records and push token, and to clear local app data.

Deleting the app is not the same as deleting your data. Removing Footies from your iPhone deletes local-only data, but does not by itself delete online League data. In version 1.0 League accounts are anonymous and are not linked to an Apple ID or email, so deleting the app without using in-app deletion also means that anonymous identity cannot be recovered. Use in-app deletion first if you want your online data removed.

Children

Footies is a general-audience football game and does not knowingly collect a child's real name, email address, precise location or advertising identifier. League display names should not contain personal contact information.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Footies features or providers change. The effective date above will be revised, and material changes will be explained in the app or release notes where appropriate.
