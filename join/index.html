How to join

  1. Open Footies on your iPhone.
  2. Go to the League tab.
  3. Choose Join a league and enter the three-letter code.

League codes are three letters and are case-insensitive.

Tapping this link does not open the app automatically. Version 1.0 does not register universal links, so the code has to be typed into Footies by hand. This page exists to carry the code, not to hand it to the app.
